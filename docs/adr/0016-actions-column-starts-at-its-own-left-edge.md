# The `actions` column starts at its own left edge, and the register's shared right edge is for values read down the column

`register-columns.ts` derives a column's alignment from its kind alone, and four kinds were aligned to the register's shared right edge: `figure`, `amount`, `standing` and `actions`. On `/admin/sessions` that produced a defect (#316). A SCHEDULED row draws five controls — Take attendance, Edit, Detail, CSV, Cancel session — while a COMPLETED row, or a CANCELLED one whose WIB day is over, draws four, because the standing control is correctly absent rather than disabled (ADR 0010) and which of Cancel or Reopen is offered is decided server-side on the WIB day (ADR 0011). With the group flushed right and the optional control rendered last, a row missing that control has all four remaining controls displaced right by its width plus one `gap-cell`. Take attendance on a closed row lands roughly where Edit sits on the row above it.

**The right edge is a property of values that are compared, not of controls that are operated.** Two Rupiah amounts, two counts, two instants and two chips are each read against their neighbours down the column, and a shared edge is what makes that comparison possible: it is why `amount` is also `whitespace-nowrap`, since half an amount is a different number. A control group is never read against the row above it. It is operated on one row at a time, and what it needs from its neighbours is the opposite property — that a given control sit at the same x on every row, so the hand knows where Edit is before the eye finds it. An edge-aligned group cannot offer that while any control in it is conditional, and the conditional control is not going away.

So `actions` leaves `RIGHT_EDGE_KINDS`. Its value box drops `md:justify-end` and its head and cell align left at every width. The slack a short row leaves now falls on the right of the group, where a ragged edge reads as absence rather than as displacement, and the four unconditional controls hold their x down the whole register.

**Three alternatives were rejected, and the reasons are the load-bearing part of this record.** Reserving the missing control's slot with an empty box keeps the right edge but requires a fixed width, and `Cancel session`, `Batalkan sesi` and `Reopen` are three different widths — any reservation is wrong in at least one locale. Putting the optional control first in DOM order is the cheapest visual fix and would let the right edge absorb the absence, but the order of the five controls is deliberate — the standing move asks first and is the fifth job — and it puts the destructive control first on the row. Doing that reorder in CSS instead, with `order`, keeps DOM and focus order intact but splits them from the visual order, trading a cosmetic defect for a WCAG 1.3.2 and 2.4.3 one. A per-slot grid, one track per control so an absent control collapses only its own track, is the most robust answer and remains open, but it requires the register to know how many action slots a row can hold — new API surface on a seam whose whole point is that a caller says only the kind.

This applies to all six registers that declare `kind: 'actions'` — Members, Applicants, Payments, Sessions, Activities and the attendance register — not only to the surface where it was reported, because the kind is the only thing a caller says about how the lattice is drawn. Below `md` nothing changes: cells already read left under their own labels there, so the defect was full-width only.

This record decides nothing about `figure`, `amount` or `standing`, which keep the shared right edge, and nothing about which controls a row draws or in what order.
