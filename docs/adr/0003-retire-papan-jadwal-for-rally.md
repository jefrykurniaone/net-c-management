# The Papan Jadwal design system is retired in full and replaced by Rally

The product shipped a deliberate design system — Papan Jadwal, the court schedule board rebuilt as software: square 2px tiles, a ruled lattice, one permitted green, state carried by the form of six marks, one type family at one width. Two delivery runs (#29 member surfaces, #30 admin registers) were built on it and it was internally consistent. On 2026-08-30 the owner decided the product should look like a contemporary sports-club SaaS identity instead (the Playbypoint case study by Afternow): a near-black green and a pale lime as the brand pair, a bright green for actions, warm off-white grounds, rounded cards on soft shadows, condensed heavy uppercase headlines, thin-line patterns, and photography. Four of Papan Jadwal's load-bearing rules — square corners, no shadow, one green, form-carried marks — are the opposite of that reference, so the system is replaced rather than re-skinned. The replacement is named **Rally**, an internal name that never reaches user-facing copy, and is specified in the run `rally` (specs #142–#146) and the rewritten `DESIGN.md`.

**Considered options.** *Re-skin only* — swap colours and keep the rules — was rejected because a product with rounded cards and lime surfaces would violate the document it claims to follow, and a design system whose rules the product breaks is worse than none. *Keep the six mark forms and change everything else* was rejected by the owner in favour of colour-plus-label chips; the accessibility obligation (WCAG 1.4.1) is met by making the text label mandatory on every chip and testing for it, not by the shape. *Keep the ruled lattice and the shared Slot Cell / Register seams* was rejected in favour of card grids on member surfaces and tables inside cards on admin surfaces, with each surface composing its own card; the server-side resolvers that decide state and available action stay shared, so behaviour has one source even though drawing does not.

**Consequences.** Every rule in the old `DESIGN.md` is retired, including the ones that were engineering rather than taste (the One Hand Rule, the Visible Rule Rule, the Never-Bleed Rule); the new document re-states the ones that still apply in Rally's terms and lists the rest under *Retired rules* with a one-line reason each, so the reasoning survives even though the rules do not. `TESTING.md` sections asserting lattice, tile or mark geometry are marked superseded, not deleted. The 2026-08-19 decision that no Admin writes public copy is superseded for the public page only (spec #143). `Activity.icon`, dropped by #65 because nothing rendered it, returns with a renderer (spec #145) as a new decision, not a revert. Production carries a mixed state while the run lands; the foundation spec is sequenced first so that the mixed state is new tokens under old layouts, never two colour systems side by side.

Status: accepted, 2026-08-30.
