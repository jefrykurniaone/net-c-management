# A member Session card outside a week: two of ADR 0014's premises are removed, the rest of it still binds

`/sessions` stops being a week and becomes one section per Activity, each holding that Activity's upcoming Sessions as cards, three across on a desktop and one on a phone (#324). `docs/adr/0014-member-session-card-conventions.md` settled the member card conventions on `WeekSessionCard`, and justified two of its rules by facts this page removes: the width of a week-strip day column, and the week strip's duty to draw every day of its range including the empty ones. This record supersedes those two parts of 0014 and nothing else. 0014 is not edited; a superseded record keeps saying what it said when it was accepted.

**What still stands, and is not re-argued here.** The *deciding* is shared and the *drawing* is not: `resolveSessionStanding` answers what state a Session is in and `slotActionFor` answers which action a member may take on it, both resolved server-side into view data before any component is reached, so `SessionGridCard` works out no colour, no label and no permission for itself. One chip, at the footer's leading edge, never two. The action is a **sibling** of the card's link and never a child of it — a control nested inside a link is invalid markup and reaches the tab order differently in different browsers — so the card link and the Seat control are two tab stops in that order. The whole information area is the link; the footer is not, because it holds a state and a button rather than a destination. Each member surface draws its own card (ADR 0003), so this is a fourth card file rather than a shared one, and the small duplication of the standing-to-words function is the price ADR 0003 already accepted in exchange for surfaces that can differ without negotiating. 0014's sanctioned deviations for the dashboard card and the Session detail header stand untouched: those surfaces still follow it, including the header's deliberate absence of an action row and of a payment deadline.

**Superseded: the card stacks because a day column is 174px.** 0014's fourth convention forbade a chip beside a figure on one line, on the measurement that a day column is about 174px and the longest chip label this product sets is most of it. A grid cell is the same page measure divided three ways rather than seven — roughly 380px, and a full-width column on a phone — so a chip and the Seat control sit on one footer line and a chip is no longer made to wrap inside its own card. What survives the measurement is the obligation, not the layout it forced: the footer still wraps rather than overflowing, so *Belum Dipasang* beside an *Ambil & bayar* control drops to a second line at a narrow width instead of running out of the card.

**Superseded: every day is drawn, including the empty ones.** 0014 argued that the week strip must keep an empty day's column and draw a dashed empty slot in it, because skipping the day hides the one thing an Admin most needs members to notice — that nothing has been posted. This page has no days to draw. It shows posted Sessions from today forward, grouped by Activity, and an Activity with nothing upcoming keeps its section and says so in one sentence rather than yielding a card per empty day (#324). So an unposted standing slot never reaches this card: `href` and `status` are non-null on `SessionGridCardData`, there is no unlinked variant of the card, and the `unposted` note the week and dashboard cards carry is absent by construction. The neutral "not posted" standing itself is untouched in `resolveSessionStanding` — the dashboard still draws standing slots and still needs it.

**Added: what leads a card that sits under a header naming its Activity.** The date and weekday lead, and they are the card's heading element: inside one Activity's section the date is the only thing that varies between cards, so it is what tells two of them apart and what is read first. Then the time range, the venue, how full the Session is, and the footer. The Activity's name and its icon tile are not drawn — the section header carries both, and repeating them on every card spends exactly the width the wider cell was meant to buy. The Session's own title is not drawn either: the recurring generator gives every Session it creates the same title, so within one Activity's section the title is repetition and the date is the identifier. Both the Activity name and the title stay in the view data and in the card's accessible name, because a link's accessible name is read out of its section's context — the link still says which Activity and which Session it opens, and the Seat control still names the Session it acts on.

**Added: how full a Session is, is drawn on the card rather than standing in the chip's place.** 0014 let the free-Seat figure occupy the footer's leading edge where the standing was a number, which on 174px meant a card showed the figure or a chip but never both. The two answer different questions — the figure is a fact about the Session, the chip is a fact about the reader's own Seat — and a member deciding from the grid needs the figure whichever chip they are carrying. So the figure moves into the information area under the venue, drawn on every card, and the chip keeps the footer's leading edge with one chip still the ceiling. Where there is neither a chip nor an action to draw, the footer is omitted rather than drawn empty: an empty bar under a card reads as a control that failed to render.

This record decides nothing about capacity, money, or what a member may do with a Seat, all of which the server enforces under a row lock; nothing about the section header or the grid that holds these cards; and nothing about the week strip's own fate. It decides what a Session card looks like where the page is not a week, and which parts of 0014 it answers to.

Status: accepted, 2026-09-05.
